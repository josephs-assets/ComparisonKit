Credits and licenses

Comparison Kit's geometry, materials and tools are original work. Environment and data sources are credited below.

Sample data

  • world_population_worldbank.csv: The World Bank's population file (indicator SP.POP.TOTL, World Development Indicators), exactly as downloaded on 24 Nov 2026 from data.worldbank.org. License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0), credit "World Bank". It is kept raw on purpose: Csv File reads it as it is. Used by L_CK_ColumnRace_AllCountries_ClassicCity.
  • The other files in SampleData are small tables made for the demo maps.

Map Stage

Natural Earth public-domain boundary geometry supplies the country shapes: Natural Earth 1:110m Admin 0, version 5.1.1. Natural Earth data and terms of use.

Environments

  • Arid shrub: Wild Rooibos Bush, Poly Haven, CC0 (source, license). Geometry and UVs retained; alpha restored from the 2k alpha map; normal map converted to DirectX.
  • Open Plain Day terrain: Valley of the Gods, Utah. Elevation: Mapzen Terrain Tiles with United States 3DEP data, courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey. Orthophotography: USGS NAIP. US government source data in the public domain. The comparison pad is flattened; the scene is a visual backdrop, not a navigation or surveying product.
  • Open Plain Alpine terrain: Jackson Hole and the Teton front, Wyoming. Elevation: USGS 3DEP via AWS Terrain Tiles. Orthophotography: USDA NAIP via The National Map (imagery service). Public domain.
  • Open Plain Prairie terrain: Flint Hills, Kansas. Elevation: USGS 3DEP via Mapzen/AWS Terrain Tiles (registry). Orthophotography: USDA NAIP via The National Map. Public domain.
  • Ground detail for Alpine and Prairie: Grass Ground, Poly Haven, CC0 (source).
  • Environment ladder: reuses the Open Plain Day terrain and the kit's CC0 sky cubemaps.
  • Utility hut: adapted from The Shed by Poly Haven, CC0 (collection). The surrounding greenhouse and unrelated objects are excluded; a masonry back wall and a second door close the standalone hut.
  • City Street and athletics track: the street barrier is adapted from the Poly Haven concrete road barrier. Sources: concrete road barrier, asphalt 04, concrete floor 02, concrete pavement 02, urban street 03, stadium 01, running track, grass ground.
  • Ocean Surface: the swell grid, material and mooring buoy geometry are original. The buoy dimensions follow the Polyform A-7 size chart (chart). Surface detail: ambientCG Plastic011 (source). Light and sky: Poly Haven Kloofendal 43d clear.
  • Warehouse: the timber pallet (1200 x 800 x 144 mm) is original. Sources: Empty Warehouse 01, Hangar Concrete Floor, Hinoki Planks, ambientCG Metal009. Poly Haven and ambientCG assets are CC0.
  • Clothed stadium spectators: the distant spectator atlas uses the MakeHuman CC0 core figure, garments, hair, skin and poses, rendered into an atlas that ships with the plugin (license, asset packs).
  • City facade and vertical section: Poly Haven Modular Urban Apartments Facade, with clay roof tiles, brick and concrete scans; the rock section uses parts of Namaqualand Cliff 02 and Sandstone Cracks. Sources: facade, clay roof tiles, brick wall 001, namaqualand cliff 02, sandstone cracks.

Terrain and aerial imagery are adapted visual backdrops, not surveying products.

Real banknotes

The cash bundle's print is a fictional currency. Reproducing real banknotes is regulated; check the rules for your currency before publishing a video that shows one.
