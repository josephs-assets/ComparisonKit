Real-Time Race

What it shows. One continuous race whose clock follows real seconds. Racers cross the line at exactly their supplied finish times; a racer can run out of stamina and either stop (DNF) or slow down. A live leaderboard shows places, then crossing times.

Actor: CK Speed Race with Use Finish Times on. Demo maps: L_CK_RealTimeRace, L_CK_StadiumTrack and L_CK_StadiumCrowd (a 40 m training run).

Data

Each item uses these fields in the Details panel, a CKItem DataTable or a CSV:

Field Meaning
finish_time seconds from GO to the finish
stamina seconds from GO until exhaustion; zero means unlimited
exhausted_action Stop or SlowDown
value the displayed comparison value; timing comes from finish_time, so a speed or rating can still be shown 
label,value,finish_time,stamina,exhausted_action
Steady runner,1,20,0,Stop
Late sprinter,2,16,4,SlowDown

Try SampleData/timed_race.csv. With Stop, exhaustion before the finish gives a DNF and the body stays at its exhaustion distance. With SlowDown, Exhausted Speed Ratio sets the reduced pace and the starting speed is solved so the racer still crosses at exactly the supplied finish time. These are illustration rules, not a physiological simulation. Rows with a missing or invalid finish time are omitted and explained in Data Warnings.

Key settings

Field Default What it does
Use Finish Times (bUseFinishTimes) false Use each row's Finish Time and Stamina in one continuous race. Off preserves the speed-ratio presentation. (Details: Comparison | Real Time)
Finish Distance (FinishDistance) 30000 Finish-line distance in scene centimeters. This is a presentation scale; it does not change supplied times. (Details: Comparison | Real Time)
Exhausted Speed Ratio (ExhaustedSpeedRatio) 0.35 Speed after exhaustion / initial speed for Slow Down rows. Initial speed is solved to keep Finish Time exact. (Details: Comparison | Real Time)
Race End Hold Seconds (RaceEndHoldSeconds) 5 Hold the result after every racer has finished or stopped. (Details: Comparison | Real Time)
Time Skips (TimeSkips) - - (Details: Comparison | Real Time)
Finish Idle (FinishIdle) - Past the line the racers brake to a stop and stand in this loop (used when its skeleton matches the racer's mesh): the finished field waits by the line for the last runner. Empty = they run through and jog on. (Details: Comparison | Real Time)
Draw Track (bDrawTrack) true Off when the level has its own floor (the demo stages): only the lane lines and markers are drawn on it. (Details: Comparison | Track)
Draw Finish Line (bDrawFinishLine) true Timed race: the finish-line bar across the lanes. Off when the set's floor paints its own line (a dark bar lay over the stadium's white line). (Details: Comparison | Track)
Fixed Theme Set (bFixedThemeSet) false Keep a physical stadium or street set fixed in the world while the racers and camera advance. (Details: Comparison | Track)
Line Material (LineMaterial) - Lane lines, start line and distance markers (an emissive strip material reads best). (Details: Comparison | Track)
Show Leaderboard (bShowLeaderboard) false Mini leaderboard in the top-left corner while the race runs: position, name and value, the racer being filmed marked (it takes the last row when it runs further back). (Details: Comparison | Overlay)
Timed Camera Travel (TimedCameraTravel) 0.24 Real-time Multi Angle camera travel per take, as a share of frame width. Keeps finished racers moving in frame; 0 locks the camera to the racer. Does not change race distances, finish times or the legacy speed mode. (Details: Comparison | Camera)
  • Finish Distance is the scene length in centimetres; changing it keeps every supplied time and distance fraction.
  • Time Skips replace a stretch of the race with a visible "LATER" card. Set From Seconds, To Seconds and Card Seconds. Outside the cards, one playback second equals one race second. Skips are sorted and must not overlap, must be inside the race and at least 0.2 seconds long.
  • When the theme or level supplies the floor, turn Draw Track off so a second plane does not overlap it. For a stadium with its own surface set Fixed Theme Set on as well.
  • Real time has one simultaneous start: extra episodes and overtake starts are ignored with a warning. Lead Tracking uses the side camera; POV keeps the continuous race clock.
  • Timed Camera Travel adds a lateral move to each Multi Angle take (a share of the frame width, default 0.24; zero locks the camera to the racer).
  • Results: tied finishers share a place, a stopped runner appears as DNF below the finishers. The clock panel reads zero while the field lines up and starts at GO.

Blueprint

Get Race Clock, Get Racer Distance, Get Racer Place, Is Racer Exhausted and Get Racer Finish Time (-1 for DNF or invalid rows). Indices are presentation item indices. See Blueprint API.
