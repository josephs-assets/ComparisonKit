Radius Rings

What it shows. Concentric rings with exact radius ratios and labelled units: blast radii, coverage areas, distances. The camera pulls back before each larger ring appears.

Actor: CK Radius Comparison. Demo map: L_CK_RadiusRings_Pinatubo.

Supply Items, a CKItem DataTable or a label,value CSV. Values are radii, not diameters or areas. Labels keep the original units.

Key settings

Field Default What it does
Maximum Scene Radius (MaximumSceneRadius) 1800 Radius in scene centimeters of the largest datum. All other radii use their true ratio to it. (Details: Comparison | Radius)
Stroke Width (StrokeWidth) 35 Line thickness in scene centimeters. The measured radius runs to the centerline of the outline. (Details: Comparison | Radius)
Ring Segments (RingSegments) 128 Line segments around each ring. 128 keeps the outline smooth at draft and HD resolutions. (Details: Comparison | Radius)
Ring Color (RingColor) FLinearColor(0.008, 0.018, 0.04) Ink colors for the default ring material. A custom Ring Material is used unchanged. (Details: Comparison | Radius)
Focus Color (FocusColor) FLinearColor(0.008, 0.06, 0.22) - (Details: Comparison | Radius)
Show Measurement Grid (bShowMeasurementGrid) true A fixed measurement grid behind the circles, spaced at one tenth of the maximum radius. (Details: Comparison | Radius)
Follow Radius (bFollowRadius) true Follow the growing comparison. Off keeps the largest ring framed from the first frame. (Details: Comparison | Radius)
Camera Drift (CameraDrift) 0.1 Sideways camera travel in frame widths. Set to zero to lock the reading holds. (Details: Comparison | Camera)
Intro Distance Scale (IntroDistanceScale) 3.6 Opening camera distance relative to the fitted first ring. One disables the push. (Details: Comparison | Camera)
Seconds Per Ring (SecondsPerRing) 3.2 - (Details: Comparison | Timing)
End Hold Seconds (EndHoldSeconds) 5 - (Details: Comparison | Timing)
Radius View (RadiusView) Diagram Diagram: rings stand in front of the camera. Ground: true-scale rings drawn on the terrain round the centre. (Details: Comparison | Radius)
  • Maximum Scene Radius sets the size of the largest ring in centimetres; every other ring keeps its exact ratio to it.
  • Follow Radius pulls the camera back before each larger ring appears; disable it for a fixed full-range composition.
  • Stroke Width changes the visibility of the outlines; measurements refer to the centre of the stroke. Ring Segments (32 to 256) controls how round the polygon is.
  • The optional Show Measurement Grid has a spacing of one tenth of the maximum radius.
  • Camera Drift is horizontal travel in frame widths; zero locks the reading holds.
  • Zero is a point at the centre, so its ring is hidden and its value stays labelled. Negative and non-finite inputs are omitted with Data Warnings. Equal values give coincident outlines and tied ranks.

Ground view

Radius View = Ground draws true-scale rings on the terrain around the centre, with Ground Unit Centimeters (100,000 means your radii are in kilometres). Elevation Items adds heights shown after the rings, side-on, as levels on a column rising from the centre.

Field Default What it does
Ground Unit Centimeters (GroundUnitCentimeters) 100000 Ground: centimetres per data unit at true scale (100,000 = radii in kilometres). (Details: Comparison | Radius)
Ground Line Width (GroundLineWidth) 0.004 Ground: ring line width as a share of the frame width, the same on every ring at any zoom. Ring colour = the row's Color. (Details: Comparison | Radius)
Ground Pitch (GroundPitch) 50 Ground: degrees the camera looks down on the rings. (Details: Comparison | Camera)
Ground Orbit Degrees (GroundOrbitDegrees) 24 Ground: degrees the camera turns round the centre over the whole sequence. (Details: Comparison | Camera)
Elevation Items (ElevationItems) - Ground: heights shown after the rings, side-on, as levels on a column rising from the centre (an eruption column against Everest and an airliner's cruise). Values in metres; use Value Text for the label's number. Empty skips it. (Details: Comparison | Elevation)
Seconds Per Elevation (SecondsPerElevation) 3 - (Details: Comparison | Elevation)

Blueprint

Get Scene Radius returns scene centimetres for an item in presentation order. Get Ring Point returns a world-space point on its circle, including actor translation, rotation and scale; angle 0 points along local +X and 90 along local +Z.
