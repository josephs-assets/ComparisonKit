Speed Race and Speed Episodes

What it shows. Every item runs in its own lane at its true share of Fastest Screen Speed, with a 3-2-1-GO countdown, camera cuts, a leaderboard and a winner card. The camera rides each racer, slowest first.

Actor: CK Speed Race. Demo maps: L_CK_SpeedRace_StudioGlass (and _Shorts), L_CK_SpeedEpisodes_StudioGlass, L_CK_SpeedDust_StudioGlass.

Item values are the speeds. A racer's mesh keeps its real size; a skeletal mesh with a run animation that has root motion is timed to its speed (no foot sliding) and turned to run along the lane. An in-place loop plays at its own rate. The theme's set travels with the camera by itself; put any pieces of your own in Travel With Camera so they ride along too.

Key settings

Field Default What it does
Fastest Screen Speed (FastestScreenSpeed) 2500 Screen speed of the fastest racer, cm/s; every other racer moves at its true share of it. (Details: Comparison | Race)
Lane Spacing (LaneSpacing) 520 - (Details: Comparison | Race)
Racer Mesh (RacerMesh) - Racer used when a row has no Mesh: empty = a column token in the kit look. Front faces +X. (Details: Comparison | Race)
Overtake (bOvertake) false Overtake: each faster racer sets off a little later and catches the racer the camera rides with right at the hand-over, so it overtakes into frame and the camera stays with the new leader (no pan across empty track). Speeds keep their true ratios on screen; distances no longer compare. (Details: Comparison | Race)
Trail Seconds (TrailSeconds) 0.6 Streak on the ground behind every racer, as long as the distance it covers in this time (0 = none). Line Material. (Details: Comparison | Race)
Dust Amount (DustAmount) 0 Dust kicked up behind every running racer (the car-commercial payoff): puffs that rise, grow and fade over Dust Seconds; 0 = none, 1 = ten puffs per racer. A pure function of time, so scrubbing and Movie Render Queue give the same frames. (Details: Comparison | Race)
Seconds Per Item (SecondsPerItem) 5 Time the camera rides with each racer. (Details: Comparison | Timing)
Transition Seconds (TransitionSeconds) 1.2 Hand-over from one racer to the next (inside SecondsPerItem). (Details: Comparison | Timing)
Countdown Seconds (CountdownSeconds) 3 3-2-1-GO before the start (the last part of the intro). (Details: Comparison | Timing)
Camera Distance (CameraDistance) 900 Closest the camera gets, cm. (Details: Comparison | Camera)
Camera Angle (CameraAngle) 24 Degrees the camera sits behind the racer. (Details: Comparison | Camera)
Focus F Stop (FocusFStop) 2.8 f-stop of a depth of field focused on the racer being filmed (the 2D layer stays sharp); 0 = everything sharp. (Details: Comparison | Camera)
Speed Blur (SpeedBlur) 1 Motion blur that grows with the speed of the racer being filmed: at 1 the shutter runs 0.15 (slowest) to 0.6 (fastest); 0 = no motion blur. (Details: Comparison | Camera)
Show Leaderboard (bShowLeaderboard) false Mini leaderboard in the top-left corner while the race runs: position, name and value, the racer being filmed marked (it takes the last row when it runs further back). (Details: Comparison | Overlay)
Leaderboard Rows (LeaderboardRows) 5 Rows of the leaderboard (9:16, and 16:9 over the world cards, show at most 3). (Details: Comparison | Overlay)
Travel With Camera (TravelWithCamera) - Stage pieces that travel with the camera along the track (backdrop, lights, fog), so the set never runs out. (Details: Comparison | Track)

Speed Episodes

Episodes (Comparison | Camera): the race runs once per entry, each with its own Camera and an optional Title card in the Episode Gap Seconds between runs. Camera styles:

Style What it films
Multi Angle cut shots of each racer
Side one lateral tracking shot
Lead Tracking stays on the leader; starts are staggered so the lead changes
Passby a low camera on the lane edge, each racer flying past
Top Down overhead, cards facing up
POV riding with each racer: from its eyes through a socket named Camera on its mesh if it has one, else over its shoulder

A typical long-form run: Multi Angle, Passby, Top Down, POV. Demo: L_CK_SpeedEpisodes_StudioGlass.

Field Default What it does
Episodes (Episodes) - The race runs once per episode, each filmed its own way: Multi Angle -> Passby -> Top Down -> POV replays the same data as a longer video. Empty = one Multi Angle run. (Details: Comparison | Camera)
Episode Gap Seconds (EpisodeGapSeconds) 1.5 Between episodes the field lines up at the start again under the next episode's title. (Details: Comparison | Timing)

Tips

  • Focus F Stop sets the depth of field on the racer in focus (higher = more in focus); Speed Blur scales motion blur with the racer's speed (0 = off).
  • Show Leaderboard adds a top-left table of the leading racers; the racer in focus is always listed.
  • Draw Track off when your level or theme supplies the floor. Line Material is for lane lines and the finish stripe.
  • For true finish times instead of speed ratios, see Real-Time Race.
