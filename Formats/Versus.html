Versus and Versus Chapters

What it shows. Two contenders over several stat rounds. Two columns change height every round; the winner of each round lights up and the score updates. The video closes on the overall winner (tick Show Outro List for a list of who took each stat).

Actor: CK Versus. Demo maps: L_CK_Versus_StudioGlass (and _Shorts), L_CK_VersusChapters_StudioGlass.

Data

Set Contender A and Contender B (label, country flag or image, optional colour) on the actor. Stats go in Stats Table (row struct CKVersusStat), Stats Csv, or the inline Stats list, in this priority order:

stat,a,b,suffix,lower_wins
Weight,190,220, kg,False
Top speed,80,65, km/h,False

One round per row. lower_wins = True when the smaller number wins (a lap time, for example). Sample: SampleData/versus_lion_tiger.csv.

Key settings

Field Default What it does
Contender A (ContenderA) - Left contender: Label, Country (flag) or Image, optional Color. (Details: Comparison | Versus)
Contender B (ContenderB) - Right contender. (Details: Comparison | Versus)
Stats Table (StatsTable) - Row struct must be CKVersusStat. Takes priority over StatsCsv and Stats. (Details: Comparison | Versus)
Stats Csv (StatsCsv) - stat,a,b[,suffix,lower_wins] — used when StatsTable is empty. Relative to the project folder. (Details: Comparison | Versus)
Spacing (Spacing) 1000 Distance between the two column centres, cm. (Details: Comparison | Layout)
Max Height (MaxHeight) 1200 Height of the larger value of a round, cm. (Details: Comparison | Layout)
Round Seconds (RoundSeconds) 9 One stat round. (Details: Comparison | Timing)
Grow Seconds (GrowSeconds) 1.2 Columns move to the round's heights and the numbers count up over this time. (Details: Comparison | Timing)
Reveal Delay (RevealDelay) 0.35 Pause between the count finishing and the winner lighting up. (Details: Comparison | Timing)
Winner Seconds (WinnerSeconds) 3 How long the winner banner holds at the start of the outro (then the round list, if Show Outro List). (Details: Comparison | Timing)
Round Shots (bRoundShots) true Off = one slowly swaying shot. (Details: Comparison | Camera)
Camera Sway (CameraSway) 4 Slow left-right drift, degrees (0 = locked off; one-shot camera only). (Details: Comparison | Camera)

Chapters

Chapters turn the same rounds into a 60-90 s Short: Solo Seconds shows each contender alone first, Replay Seconds replays the closest round in slow motion at Replay Speed, Pov Seconds looks from the top of the overall winner's column across at the loser. Apply Short Chapters sets 3 s solo, 6 s replay at 40 % and 3 s POV, and fits the sequence; 0 turns a chapter off.

Field Default What it does
Solo Seconds (SoloSeconds) 0 Each contender alone on screen for this long before the rounds (0 = straight into head-to-head). (Details: Comparison | Chapters)
Replay Seconds (ReplaySeconds) 0 Slow-motion replay of the closest round after the rounds, this long (0 = none). (Details: Comparison | Chapters)
Replay Speed (ReplaySpeed) 0.4 Replay speed (0.4 = 40 % of real time). (Details: Comparison | Chapters)
Pov Seconds (PovSeconds) 0 The overall winner's view from the top of its column across to the loser, before the outro (0 = none). (Details: Comparison | Chapters)

Tips

  • Keep stats readable: a round every 9 s suits long form; shorten Round Seconds for Shorts.
  • Each round is cut into several shots when Round Shots is on; switch it off for one steady shot (then Camera Sway applies).
  • Chapters can create cuts under a third of a second; Fit Sequence reports them in Data Status.
