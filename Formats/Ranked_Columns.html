Ranked Column Race

What it shows. Columns rise one by one in value order, each with a flag or picture, a name card and a counting number. The camera follows the leader up to #1, then pulls back over the finished row. Use it for top-10 lists and any "X, Y and Z by the numbers".

Actor: CK Comparison Row, Item Shape = Column.

Demo maps

  • L_CK_StudioGlass, L_CK_ArenaNight: the default light and dark sets.
  • L_CK_ColumnRace_ClassicCity and L_CK_ColumnRace_ClassicCity_Headline: classic data look with the cards on the column faces (Card Placement = Face; the Headline variant uses Face Preset = Headline). On a theme with flag panels (Arena Night) the panel sits under a face card, so the value digits stay on the dark column face and read over any flag.
  • L_CK_ColumnRace_AllCountries_ClassicCity: every country, read from the raw World Bank file.
  • L_CK_FlagBearer_ClassicCity: a figure carries the flag on top of each column.
  • L_CK_ToppleFinale_ClassicCity: the columns topple one after another at the end.
  • L_CK_StudioGlass_Shorts: the 9:16 copy.

Key settings

Item Shape (ItemShape) Column - (Details: Comparison | Layout)
Scale Mode (ScaleMode) Linear How a value maps to height (Column and Mesh shapes). Keep Linear for true-scale comparisons; Auto picks Logarithmic when the values span over 40x. Rows without any value (a plain list) stand on equal columns of Max Height and show no number or rank. (Details: Comparison | Layout)
Max Height (MaxHeight) 1600 Height of the largest item, cm. (Details: Comparison | Layout)
Gap (Gap) 400 Space between neighbouring items, cm. (Details: Comparison | Layout)
Entrance (Entrance) Cycle - (Details: Comparison | Timing)
Card Placement (CardPlacement) Above Column shape: Face puts the card on the column's front face near its top (classic data look). The column then grows at least tall enough to hold the card. (Details: Comparison | Look | Column)
Face Preset (FacePreset) Classic Face card layout: Classic (name, value, caption, rank) or Headline (flag or picture on top, the rank as a big headline). (Details: Comparison | Look | Column)
Flag Style (FlagStyle) Pole - (Details: Comparison | Look | Flag)
Flag Carrier (bFlagCarrier) false Pole style: a figure stands on the item's top beside the pole, its hand at the pole, playing its idle loop (the flag bearer on the lead wagon). The pole and flag grow with the figure. (Details: Comparison | Look | Flag)
Topple Finale (bToppleFinale) false Closing payoff: once the outro camera has pulled back, the items topple one after another (row order) and crash down with a dust burst and a jolt of the camera. Scripted, not simulated: every render of a frame is identical. The outro lasts until the last item has landed and its dust has settled. (Details: Comparison | Finale)
Field Default What it does
Rank Order (RankOrder) HighestFirst Which end of the scale is #1. Equal values share a rank (#3, #3, #5). (Details: Comparison | Data)
Max Items (MaxItems) 0 Keep only the top N items by Rank Order (0 = all). (Details: Comparison | Data)
Sort Ascending (bSortAscending) true Reveal order: ascending shows the smallest first, so with Highest First ranking the video ends on #1. (Details: Comparison | Data)
Count Seconds (CountSeconds) 1 Seconds a value takes to arrive; 0 = it appears whole. The compact unit stays the final one while it runs. (Details: Comparison | Values)
Value Reveal (ValueReveal) Roll How a value arrives: Roll (random digits lock left to right), Count (runs up from zero) or Instant. (Details: Comparison | Values)
Total Label (TotalLabel) - Group total over the closing shot: this label and the sum of every value ("Combined 1.2 T"). Empty = none. (Details: Comparison | Overlay)

Timing of the camera and the payoff:

Field Default What it does
Seconds Per Item (SecondsPerItem) 4.5 Camera travel time from one item to the next. It never stops. (Details: Comparison | Timing)
Dwell Strength (DwellStrength) 0.35 The camera slows as it passes each item and makes the time up between items (0 = constant speed). 0.35: 0.65x speed over an item, 1.35x between two; it never stops. (Details: Comparison | Timing)
Brake Seconds (BrakeSeconds) 2 The camera eases out over this time before stopping on #1. (Details: Comparison | Timing)
Grow Seconds (GrowSeconds) 1.1 Length of an item's entrance animation. (Details: Comparison | Timing)
Final Reveal Seconds (FinalRevealSeconds) 6 The #1 payoff: #1 builds slowly while the camera climbs it from the base to its card, then pulls back over the hold. 0 = #1 enters like the others. (Details: Comparison | Timing)
Recap Seconds (RecapSeconds) 0 Recap before the outro: a cut back to the smallest item, then the camera sweeps the whole row up to #1, each card lighting up as it passes (0 = none). (Details: Comparison | Timing)
Giant Foot Reveal (bGiantFootReveal) false Giant Foot: #1 already stands when the camera reaches it; the camera pushes in low on its base (its top far out of frame), holds a beat, then cranes up and back to show all of it (Final Reveal Seconds). Off: #1 builds while the camera climbs. (Details: Comparison | Timing)

Tips

  • Scale Mode: keep Linear for honest comparisons. Logarithmic and Auto exist for data that spans several orders of magnitude; Auto picks Logarithmic when the largest value is over 40 times the smallest.
  • Press Apply Long Form Timing or Apply Shorts Timing for a ready pace, then Fit Sequence.
  • Recap Seconds adds a cut back to the smallest item and one sweep along the whole row to #1, each card lighting up as the camera passes.
  • Act Cards (Comparison | Overlay) put a title card over an item as the camera reaches it, for example "The supertalls / Over 600 m".
  • Total Label adds a closing card with the sum of every value ("Combined 24,312 t"). Empty means none.
  • Long lists: set Max Items, and read Data Warnings for what was left out.
