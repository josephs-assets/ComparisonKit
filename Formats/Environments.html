Environment demos

Besides the Studio Glass set, the kit ships photographic and measured environments. Open a level under Plugins > Comparison Kit > Maps and press Play. Each demo starts automatically. Select the comparison actor in the Outliner to change its data. After changing data through Blueprint, call Rebuild; use Set Time to replay a particular moment.

Map What to try
L_CK_CityStreet Compare the height of a road barrier, a covered car and a bus against the same pavement and apartment modules. The row values are mesh heights in metres. The wet asphalt material exposes Wetness.
L_CK_OceanSurface Compare groups of two and six mooring buoys. In the theme's Ocean settings change Amplitude in centimetres or Period in seconds. Set amplitude to zero for flat water.
L_CK_StadiumTrack Follow three pacers in a 40 m training run. The sample finish times are 10.8, 12.0 and 13.4 seconds. Change Finish Time on one row, rebuild and compare the leaderboard at the finish.
L_CK_StadiumCrowd The same 40 m comparison with a populated home straight: 485 stationary audience figures (two-sided cards composed for track-distance views), terraces and access stairs as meshes. Select the Stadium Track theme to use the empty oval.
L_CK_Accumulator, L_CK_Lap Earning rates and lap times in the Warehouse set. See Accumulator and Lap.
L_CK_OpenPlainAlpine, L_CK_OpenPlainPrairie A true-scale car and person example over open terrain. Each has a matching sequence (LS_CK_OpenPlainAlpine, LS_CK_OpenPlainPrairie) with four inspection views.
L_CK_UtilityHut The prairie utility hut with LS_CK_UtilityHut and four inspection views.

Water comparison

Choose Unit Layout = Field, enable Float Field Units, then set Unit Draft Cm for the mesh pivot (the demo uses 46 cm). Get Ocean Surface At World Point returns a transform at the sampled surface whose up axis is the water normal for your world position and presentation time. The wave and the buoy readouts follow the comparison clock during Play, Sequencer and reverse seeks.

Track race on a level surface

Set Fixed Theme Set on and Draw Track off. The stadium has eight 1.22 m lanes, and the material marks the near straight for a 40 m drill. Hold Last Frame keeps the completed comparison on screen at the end of normal Play.

Open terrain themes

Enable Build Set and choose DA_CK_Theme_OpenPlainDay for the desert, DA_CK_Theme_OpenPlainAlpine for the mountain valley or DA_CK_Theme_OpenPlainPrairie for rolling grassland. Each terrain covers an 81 km square around a flattened comparison pad and uses a 100 km sky; it is a visual set, not a geographic simulation.

Utility hut

Drag Environments/UtilityHut/BP_CK_UtilityHut into your own level for the complete hut. Move, rotate and scale the actor normally; its 195 fasteners share mesh instances. It adds no camera or lights. The pivot is on the ground and the front faces +X. The assembly measures about 4.41 x 3.32 x 3.49 metres with simple box collision. It is closed set dressing: the doors do not open and it is not a walkable interior.
